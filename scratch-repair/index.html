We remove all road wear and cosmetic scratches from your vehicle. From door panels to bonnets, we restore your car's pristine appearance.

Our Scratch Repair Services

We use advanced techniques and high-quality materials to remove scratches from any part of your vehicle.

Painted Panel Scratch Removal

Expert removal of scratches on doors, bonnet, trunk, and fenders. We blend paint seamlessly for an invisible repair.

Clear Coat Polish & Restore

We polish out light scratches and swirl marks, restoring your vehicle's original shine without repainting.

Wheel & Alloy Scratch Repair

Specialized treatment for scratches on alloy wheels and rims. We restore the mirror finish of your wheels.

Plastic & Trim Scratch Removal

Remove scratches from plastic bumpers, door trims, and mirrors. We use specialized compounding techniques.

Full Panel Scratch Restoration

Complete scratch removal across an entire panel. We ensure a flawless, seamless finish that matches the original paint.

Our Scratch Removal Process

We use a systematic approach to ensure all scratches are effectively removed.

Step 1: Visual Inspection

We thoroughly inspect your vehicle to identify all scratches and determine the best treatment for each.

Step 2: Surface Cleaning

We thoroughly clean and degrease the affected area to ensure proper adhesion of our products.

Step 3: Scratch Assessment

We assess scratch depth and severity to determine whether polishing, compounding, or paint blending is needed.

Step 4: Treatment Application

We apply the appropriate treatment - polishing compounds, micro-abrasives, or color-matching paint as needed.

Step 5: Buffing & Polishing

We buff the treated area to blend it seamlessly with the surrounding paint, revealing a smooth, scratch-free finish.

Step 6: Quality Inspection

We inspect your vehicle under proper lighting to ensure all scratches have been completely removed.
