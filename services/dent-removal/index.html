Our Dent Removal Services

Paintless Dent Removal (PDR)

We use specialized tools to remove dents from metal surfaces without any paint. Perfect for door dings, hail damage, and small impacts.

Small Dent Repair

Larger dents that cannot be done paintlessly will receive a gentle fill, sand and repaint to match your original finish perfectly.

Rocket Dent Removal

Rocket dent removal is our trademark technique using a specialized vacuum-powered tool to pull dents out from the inside of the panel.

Door Dent Removal

The most common type of car dent. We specialize in removing door dings, whether from shopping carts, parking structures, or minor impacts.

Why Choose Our Dent Removal Service?
