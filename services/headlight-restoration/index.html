Professional Headlight Restoration

Revive cloudy, yellowed headlights with our 4-stage restoration process. Improve visibility, safety, and your car's appearance.

Why Your Headlights Need Restoration

Over time, exposure to UV rays and weather causes headlights to become cloudy, yellowed, and faded. This not only affects your car's appearance but also reduces visibility for you and other drivers.

Poor Visibility

Cloudy headlights scatter light, reducing your ability to see the road at night and in poor weather conditions.

Legal Requirement

Faded or cloudy headlights reduce light output, making your vehicle non-compliant with safety regulations.

Safety Risk

Damaged headlights increase the risk of accidents by reducing your ability to see hazards and be seen by others.

Worn Appearance

Yellowed headlights significantly reduce your car's value and give it an aged appearance.

Our 4-Stage Headlight Restoration Process

We use advanced technology to restore your headlights to like-new clarity, improving both safety and aesthetics.

Stage 1: Cleaning & Degreasing

We thoroughly clean the headlight to remove dirt, road grime, and old sealant before beginning restoration.

Stage 2: Sanding (400-6000 Grit)

We sand the cloudy surface progressively using high-grit sandpaper, starting from 400 grit and working up to 6000+ grit.

Stage 3: Polishing

We use micro-abrasives and polishing compounds to restore optical clarity and gloss finish.

Stage 4: UV Protection Coating

We apply a high-quality clear coating that protects against UV rays and extends the restoration's effectiveness.
