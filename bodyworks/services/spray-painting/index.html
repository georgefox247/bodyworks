Our Paint Process

We follow a proven 5-step process to ensure your vehicle looks better than new.

Step 1: Assessment & Preparation

We assess your vehicle, discuss your requirements, and prepare the surface by removing old paint, filling holes, and sanding.

Step 2: Masking & Taping

All areas around your paint job are meticulously masked and taped to protect them from overspray.

Step 3: Priming

We apply a high-quality primer to create an even surface for the paint and ensure maximum adhesion.

Step 4: Base Coat Application

We apply your chosen color in multiple thin, controlled coats using precision spray guns for a smooth finish.

Step 5: Clear Coat & Drying

We apply a protective clear coat for gloss and UV protection, then carefully dry your vehicle in our climate-controlled facility.

Step 6: Quality Check & Delivery

We inspect your vehicle thoroughly, remove all masking, and deliver it to you in pristine condition.
