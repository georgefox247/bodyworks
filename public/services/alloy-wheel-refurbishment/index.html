Our Wheel Refurbishment Process

We use a systematic approach to ensure your alloy wheels are restored to their best condition.

Step 1: Wheel Removal & Cleaning

We carefully remove the wheel and thoroughly clean it to ensure proper preparation.

Step 2: Rust & Oxidation Removal

We use chemical rust removers and wire brushes to remove surface corrosion from the alloy.

Step 3: Surface Degreasing

We degrease and polish the wheel surface to prepare it for treatment.

Step 4: Polishing & Compound Application

We apply polishing compounds and buff wheels to restore mirror shine.

Step 5: Bonded Coating Application

We apply a protective bonded coating for long-lasting corrosion and scratch protection.

Step 6: Final Inspection

We inspect each wheel under proper lighting to ensure it meets our quality standards before reinstalling.
